Fifteen days, two islands, one rental car, one campervan and about 2,900 km of scenery that made us pull over every ten minutes. This is how two Indonesians living in Singapore finally did New Zealand, from Auckland all the way down to Milford Sound and back to Christchurch.

![](/assets/images/uploads/1790313031187-img_4405.jpg)

## ✨ The trip in numbers

- 🗓️ **15 days** + one overnight flight, mid-October (NZ spring)
- 🚗 **~2,900 km** driven: car up north, campervan down south
- 🚐 **6 nights** in a van
- ⛴️ **1 ferry** across Cook Strait, **1 scenic train** over the Southern Alps
- 💸 **~SGD 5.1k per person**, flights included

## 🗺️ The route: one big loop

Fly into Auckland, drive south through the geothermal North Island, sail to the South Island, then campervan the West Coast, lakes and fiords before looping back to Christchurch.

## 🌋 North Island: steam, volcanoes and a windy capital (Days 1–5)

We landed in Auckland after lunch and went straight to the airport hotel instead of driving jet-lagged. Best decision of the trip (and we got a free room upgrade).

**Rotorua** smells like boiled eggs, and you stop noticing by dinner. The next day was an easy one: a short drive to **Taupō** and the electric-blue **Huka Falls**, which is free and only a few minutes' walk from the car park.

Day 4 was the **Desert Road**, with Mt Ruapehu and Tongariro watching over us all the way to **Wellington**. Then an early **Interislander ferry** through the Marlborough Sounds, and a coastal drive past **Ohau Point**, where fur seals nap on the rocks right beside the road.

## 🚐 South Island: six nights in a campervan (Days 6–13)

In Christchurch we swapped the car for a Maui campervan and immediately detoured for **Sheffield Pies** (buy an extra for the road). Then over **Arthur's Pass**, past the giant boulders of **Castle Hill**, to a quiet lakeside DOC campsite for just SGD 21.


![](/assets/images/uploads/1790313086680-2d4155c9-658b-4279-a665-319dfc7a8237_1_201_a.jpeg)


The days that followed were the reason we came:

- 🧊 **Franz Josef Glacier** walk in the morning, **#ThatWanakaTree** at golden hour the same evening
- 🍔 **Queenstown**: Fergburger (worth the queue) and the Skyline Luge (ride it more than once)
- 🏞️ **Milford Sound**: a 4-hour drive, a 2-hour cruise among waterfalls and seals, then Mirror Lakes on the way back
- 🍣 **Lake Pukaki**: fresh salmon sashimi at the foot of Aoraki/Mt Cook, then our wildest night: no power, no signal, a sky full of stars
- ⭐ **Hooker Valley Track** to a glacier lake, then stargazing in Tekapo's Dark Sky Reserve

## 💰 What it really cost

We spent **SGD 10,209 for two** (about SGD 5,100 each) in October 2023. For 2026 we'd budget **around SGD 11,260** because of higher visa fees, the new tourist levy and pricier vans. Flights and wheels (car, campervan, ferry, petrol) made up 67% of the total.

The full guide breaks down every dollar by category and day, plus 8 ways to spend less (tip: book the TranzAlpine direct, we overpaid by over SGD 350).

## 🛂 Heads-up for Indonesian passport holders

You need a proper **visitor visa**, not the NZeTA, even if you live in Singapore. In 2026 it's from NZD 441 per person, including the NZD 100 visitor levy, and takes about 2–4 weeks. Apply 6–8 weeks before you fly and keep bookings refundable until it's approved.

*Fees and rules change, so always confirm on immigration.govt.nz. We're travellers, not immigration advisers.*

## 💡 5 things we wish we knew

1. **Declare all your food.** Snacks, sambal, instant noodles, abon, everything. Not declaring means an instant NZD 400 fine.
2. **Check the van's battery at pick-up.** If it isn't full, make your first night a powered holiday park.
3. **Fuel up before remote stretches.** Haast Pass and the Milford road have little fuel and no signal. Download offline maps.
4. **Leave Queenstown by 7 am for Milford.** Save the photo stops for the drive back so you don't miss your cruise.
5. **Pack soft bags.** Campervan storage is tiny, and duffels squash into lockers far better than suitcases.
