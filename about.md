We're **The Candid Duo** — a couple (+1 toddler) who got tired of the highlight reel.

Somewhere between the perfectly-lit hotel shots and the "top 10 things to do" lists, we felt like travel had lost its honesty. So we started documenting ours: the real costs, the wrong turns, the buses we missed, and the tiny unplanned moments that turned into the best stories.

Based in Singapore, we cover everything from slow island loops and mountain road trips to spiritual journeys like our first Umroh. Some of it is polished. A lot of it isn't. That's the point.

## What you'll find here

- **Stories** — long-form travel journals with the details other blogs skip: what things actually cost, what we'd do differently, and what we'd fly back for tomorrow.
- **Vlogs** — the unedited-feeling version of the trip over on [YouTube]({{ site.social.youtube }}) and [TikTok]({{ site.social.tiktok }}).
- **The podcast** — honest conversations about the reality of full-time-ish travel, money, and doing this as a duo.

## Why "candid"?

Because the best moments are rarely the posed ones. We'd rather show you the sunrise we almost slept through than a version of travel that doesn't exist.
